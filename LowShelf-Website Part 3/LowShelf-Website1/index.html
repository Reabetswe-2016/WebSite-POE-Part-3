Welcome to LowShelf!

Quality Shelving and Racking Solutions

Lowveld Shelving and Racking is a South African family-owned company based in White River, Mpumalanga. With more than 30 years of experience in shopfitting, the company provides industrial and warehouse shelving and racking solutions.

LowShelf provides storage solutions suitable for warehouses, factories, workshops, garages and other industrial environments.
