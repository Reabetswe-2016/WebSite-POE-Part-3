About LowShelf

Lowveld Shelving and Racking, also known as LowShelf, is a South African family-owned business based near White River, Mpumalanga.

With more than 30 years of experience in shopfitting, LowShelf provides shelving, racking, storage and shopfitting solutions for different business environments.

Our Services

  • Warehouse storage solutions
  • Shopfitting solutions
  • Retail shelving
  • Custom storage solutions
